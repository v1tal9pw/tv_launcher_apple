RemoteButtonMapper adapts the remote-button workflow and tap dispatcher from
ButtonMapper.java in the user-supplied tvOS-Launcher-2.1.5-ru-desktop1.zip,
package com.techgenius.tvoslauncher, distributed under Apache License 2.0.
The complete license is in tvos-launcher-LICENSE.txt beside this file.

Modified for this project: preference storage, launcher actions, existing
accessibility service integration, Russian UI, and separate reporting of the
system Home assignment and optional Home-key interception. Local ADB and
stock-launcher disabling were not imported.
